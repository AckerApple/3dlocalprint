How 3D Pet Print Works

Get StartedBack Home
Section 1

🔐Sign in first

Account

Sign in so your pet print request, uploaded photos, proof previews, messages, and print status can stay connected to you.

This also helps us keep each request organized as it moves from photo review to modeling, approval, printing, and pickup or delivery planning.

3D Pet Print is operated by 3D Local Print LLC as a specialized pet-focused experience.

Section 2

📂Upload pet photos

Photos

Upload clear, well-lit photos of your dog, cat, or other pet. Front and side angles are especially helpful when you have them.

Include markings, ears, tail, favorite poses, and any notes about the type of keepsake you want.

Multiple photos help us understand personality and practical modeling details before we begin.

Section 3

✅Review your proof

Approval

After reviewing your photos, we prepare preview details for the custom pet print before production.

You will receive proof information to approve, with room for practical adjustments before we commit the design to printing.

The goal is to make sure the print direction matches the pet, pose, and keepsake style you expected.

Section 4

🖨️Printing your pet

Print

Once approved, your pet keepsake is 3D printed, checked, and prepared for finishing, pickup, or delivery planning.

Possible keepsakes include custom pet figurines, memorial pieces, ornaments, desk buddies, and gifts for pet lovers.

You can track the request through the process as upload, proof, and status tools are added.

Ready to make your pet printable?

Start with your favorite photos and a few notes about the keepsake you want to create.
